Cohort Retention Summary

Group customers by when they started and see how many come back.

Works offline - nothing leaves this device

1. Load your activity

One row per order or activity. Columns it understands: Customer ID, Date (of the order or activity) and optionally Signup date. Without a signup date, a customer's first activity decides their cohort. Use IDs rather than names or e-mail addresses where you can.

2. Retention by cohort

Each cell is the share of that cohort active in that period. Blank cells have not happened yet.
